# Portfolio Rebrand Brief

## Objective

Completely redesign and reposition my portfolio from a traditional UX/Product Design portfolio into a Creative Technologist portfolio.

This is not a portfolio about wireframes, user flows, personas, or generic UX process.

When ideas are difficult to explain, I build them.

Creative Technologist and Product Designer leveraging AI, code, design systems, and cognitive psychology to transform emerging concepts into tangible product experiences.

I am not positioning myself as:

- UX Manager
- UX Researcher
- Design Systems Architect

Those are things I've done.

I am positioning myself as:

- Creative Technologist
- AI Product Designer
- Product Visionary
- Interactive Prototyper
- Experience Builder

My value is not creating documentation.

---

# Portfolio Philosophy

Traditional portfolios explain the work.

This portfolio demonstrates the work.

Every major project should include:

1. The problem
2. What I built
3. How I built it
4. What changed because of it

Avoid long UX case studies.

Avoid walls of text.

Use video, motion, interaction, and demonstrations whenever possible.

Show, don't tell.

---

# Design Principles

## Principle 1

Most teams explain ideas with slides.

I build them.

## Principle 2

Working software is more valuable than static mockups.

## Principle 3

AI is not a feature.

AI is a product development accelerator.

## Principle 4

The future is easier to understand when people can interact with it.

## Principle 5

Design, AI, code, and psychology are inseparable.
