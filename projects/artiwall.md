# Arti Wall

## Project Overview

Arti Wall is a next-generation operating room intelligence platform designed to provide surgical teams with a centralized, contextual, and AI-assisted view of critical information before, during, and after a procedure.

The project originated from a leadership vision. My responsibility was to transform an early concept into a tangible, interactive experience that stakeholders, engineers, surgeons, and operating room staff could understand, evaluate, and rally around.

The resulting prototype became a funded roadmap initiative with an MVP targeted for release in 2027.

## The Challenge

Operating rooms are information dense environments.

Critical information is spread across multiple systems:

- Preference cards
- PACS imaging
- MRI scans
- X-rays
- Procedure information
- Equipment status
- Workflow progress

Staff frequently switch between systems and physical assets while attempting to maintain situational awareness.

Leadership wanted to explore whether a large ambient display could centralize these workflows while adapting to the needs of multiple clinical roles.

---

## Approach

Rather than creating static mockups, I built an interactive product vision.

The experience was designed around four primary personas:

- Surgeon
- Circulating Nurse
- Scrub Technician
- Anesthesia Provider

Two operating room workflows were explored:

- Synergy.net connected environments
- Standalone operating room environments

I leveraged AI technologies to move beyond traditional UX demonstrations and create a prototype that users could actively engage with.

---

## AI Integration

One of the most significant contributions was integrating LLM-powered voice interactions into the prototype.

Users could:

- Ask questions
- Navigate content
- Manipulate interface states
- Retrieve information using natural language

This transformed the prototype from a presentation into a conversational product experience.
