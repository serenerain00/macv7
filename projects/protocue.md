# ProtoCue

## Project Overview

ProtoCue was born out of a problem I encountered repeatedly while building AI-assisted, code-first product experiences.

As product design increasingly shifts from static mockups toward interactive coded prototypes, traditional feedback workflows begin to break down.

Stakeholders leave comments in:

- Slack
- Microsoft Teams
- Email
- Screenshots
- Meetings
- Documents
- Text messages

Feedback becomes fragmented and disconnected from the actual experience being reviewed.

ProtoCue was created to solve this problem.

## The Problem

Traditional review tools assume that Figma is the source of truth.

That assumption is becoming increasingly outdated.

Modern product development is rapidly shifting toward:

- AI-generated prototypes
- Interactive demos
- Coded proof-of-concepts
- Production-like experiences

The problem is that stakeholder feedback remains trapped inside disconnected communication channels.

As a result:

- Context gets lost
- Decisions become difficult to trace
- Feedback becomes difficult to prioritize
- Product teams spend time reconstructing conversations

---

## The Insight

I realized something important:

The future source of truth is not the mockup.

The source of truth is the experience.

If stakeholders are reviewing a live coded prototype, feedback should be attached directly to the experience itself.

Not to screenshots.

Not to emails.

Not to meeting notes.

---

## The Solution

ProtoCue allows users to submit contextual feedback directly on interactive experiences.

Users provide a URL to a prototype or live experience.

Stakeholders can:

- Navigate the experience
- Leave comments
- Attach feedback to specific screens
- Capture context automatically
- Centralize conversations

This creates a single location where product feedback can be reviewed and prioritized.
